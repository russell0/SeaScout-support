Delete SeaScout local data

SeaScout does not create a server account. Your saved pins and imported region data are kept on your iPhone or paired Apple Watch.

  1. Delete saved pins or imported regions from the corresponding SeaScout screen on each device. A paired device may retain its own copy.
  2. Remove SeaScout from the iPhone to remove its local app data.
  3. Remove the Watch app from your Apple Watch if a Watch copy remains.

Turning off location permission in your device’s Settings prevents new location access; it does not erase saved marks. Device backups and restoration are managed through your Apple device and backup settings.

If you need help locating data, email russellhanson@gmail.com. Include only the details needed to identify the issue.
