Delete SeaScout local data

SeaScout does not create a server account. Your mode preference, saved pins and imported region data are kept on your iPhone or paired Apple Watch.

  1. Manage saved sites and private points in Saved. For imported regions on iPhone, open Settings → Offline sites and imports in iPhone mode, or Prepare in Watch mode, then use Remove from this device. A paired device may retain its own copy.
  2. Remove SeaScout from the iPhone to remove its local app data.
  3. Remove the Watch app from your Apple Watch if a Watch copy remains.

Changing between iPhone and Watch modes preserves data; it is not a deletion command. iPhone mode stops new automatic Watch transfers, but existing Watch copies and pending requests remain. Switching back to Watch mode may resume delivery.

Turning off location permission in your device’s Settings prevents new location access; it does not erase saved marks. Device backups and restoration are managed through your Apple device and backup settings.
