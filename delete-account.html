Delete SeaScout local data

The current SeaScout pilot does not create a server account. Your saved pins and imported region data are kept on your iPhone or paired Apple Watch.

  1. Delete saved pins or imported regions from the corresponding SeaScout screen.
  2. Remove SeaScout from the iPhone to remove its local app data.
  3. Remove the watch app from the Watch app on iPhone if a watch copy remains.

If you need help locating data, email russellhanson@gmail.com. Include only the details needed to identify the issue.
