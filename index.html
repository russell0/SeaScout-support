SeaScout • current pilot

Navigation for divers, on your wrist.

SeaScout helps you browse saved reefs and wrecks on iPhone and Apple Watch. The current pilot is designed for local testing with paired devices.

Need help?

For installation, map, or pairing support, email russellhanson@gmail.com. Include your iPhone and watchOS versions and a short description of what happened.

Current pilot limitations

  • SeaScout is a navigation aid and reference tool. It is not an emergency service, dive computer, or substitute for a certified plan, chart, compass, or local conditions.
  • Underwater GPS is unavailable. Location accuracy depends on the device, reception, and conditions.
  • Map imagery and basemap availability depend on Apple MapKit and network access. Saved pins can remain available on the device when the base map is unavailable.
