Use SeaScout on your iPhone

  1. On Explore, choose iPhone. New installations start in this mode; no Watch or account is required. The optional Continue on iPhone button records that choice.
  2. Open Map. Search for a destination such as Neptune Memorial Reef, or pan, pinch and use plus/minus. Type icons distinguish reefs, wrecks, springs, caves, lakes and other records. Tap a numbered cluster to expand it; closely spaced points can open in a list.
  3. Choose a type or Saved filter. Show all matches fits the results. Sites in view lists up to 100 matches nearest the map center, with distances measured from that center.
  4. Tap Nearby for a one-time foreground location lookup. Your filters remain active. Manual browsing works without location permission and does not start continuous location updates.
  5. Select a pin or result, read Details, then choose Start surface guidance. You do not need to retype the site's coordinates. Allow location access when requested. The current usable GPS position supplies distance and target bearing; measured compass heading is separate.
  6. Use Save site for a bookmark, or Saved → Add private coordinate for a point you know. End guidance explicitly when finished.

In iPhone mode, Explore, Map, Saved and Settings remain available; Watch transfer buttons and Prepare are hidden. Use Settings → Offline sites and imports for saved source records and region imports. Switch to Apple Watch on Explore or in Settings whenever you want the Watch workflow. The choice persists, and switching preserves your saved content. Existing Watch preparation may select Watch mode once during migration; your explicit choice takes priority.

Choose a map layer

Standard and Satellite use Apple Maps. Topographic adds USGS land contours in covered U.S. areas. Seafloor relief adds NOAA shaded terrain for geographic context; its colors are not measured water depth or clearance. Read the source and coverage note in the layer chooser. Custom USGS/NOAA imagery needs network access or available cache and requests detail only through zoom 16. Zoom out or switch layers when imagery is unavailable. Location packs contain records, not offline imagery; the Watch retains its Apple basemap.

Prepare a destination for Apple Watch

  1. On Explore or in Settings, choose Apple Watch. The Prepare tab and Watch actions become available.
  2. Search for Neptune Memorial Reef, open its details and tap Select for Watch. Open SeaScout on the paired Watch, then wait for Selected on Watch on the phone. A queued selection is not confirmation.
  3. On Watch, open Prepared target, choose the site and tap Start surface guidance. Allow location access when asked.

Watch guidance shows distance, true target bearing, a separate compass heading and a north-up map that fits your position and destination. T means true north; M means magnetic north. During GPS gaps, Last GPS identifies historical position and range with their age. Keep the Watch above water for a fresh position. The map's ellipsis opens session controls, including End navigation.

Switching back to iPhone mode stops new automatic Watch transfers and preserves pending requests and existing copies. Returning to Watch mode can resume pending delivery. Switching modes does not erase data already on the Watch or recall a transfer already delivered.

Load an area for your Watch before your trip

  1. In Watch mode, open Prepare → Choose a location pack.
  2. Choose Hollywood & South Florida (1,015 records), Key Largo & Upper Keys (250), Key West & Lower Keys (220), Florida springs & caves (340), Biscayne National Park (90), or U.S. lakes & reservoirs (300), then tap Load to Watch.
  3. Open SeaScout on the paired Watch and wait for Ready on Watch in Prepare. Send again and Retry queued transfers retry delivery. A request is not a delivery receipt.
  4. On Watch, Offline sites shows accepted areas and the prepared target. Requested areas remain listed after restarting; the bundled catalogue alone does not populate your prepared areas.

Area counts overlap: all 250 Upper Keys records are included in Hollywood & South Florida. Lower Keys has 220 mooring records at 24 source locations, not 220 separate dive sites. Biscayne records are buoy positions, not wreck centers. Loading an area saves coordinates and source details; it does not download map imagery.

Find lakes and freshwater features

Try Lakes & reservoirs, or search Lake Okeechobee, Lake Powell or Lake Tahoe. The 300-record USGS collection represents features across 48 states; it is a selected catalogue, not every U.S. lake or a ranking of the largest lakes. Lake reference point · Not a launch or entry means an official representative feature coordinate, generally an approximate center, rather than a boat ramp, shoreline approach or dive site. Water level, salinity, depth, access and conditions remain unknown unless the source says otherwise.

For Florida freshwater features, search Ginnie Spring or Orange Grove, or choose Spring, Cave or Sinkhole. The 340-record collection contains 333 spring records, six cave-associated spring positions and Orange Grove sinkhole. Details retain agency names, aliases, comments and linked operator or state-park classification information. These records do not establish access or diving suitability; a cave label does not describe a surveyed entrance, cave passage or underwater route.

Know what the map shows

  • The 1,965 reference records comprise 765 FWC artificial-reef deployments, 470 NOAA moorings, 340 Florida Geological Survey freshwater records, 90 NPS Biscayne moorings and 300 USGS GNIS lake/reservoir points. These are not 1,965 unique or independently verified dive sites. Source limitations remain applicable.
  • Surface guidance is also available for private coordinates, valid saved surface marks and selected charted points after accepting source terms. A saved boat mark records the boat's position at that time; it is not live boat tracking.
  • SeaScout provides surface guidance and geographic reference. It is not a dive computer, emergency service, official nautical chart or safe-route planner. Straight-line bearings do not account for land, shoals, restricted water or current conditions.
  • GPS is unavailable underwater. This candidate does not supply live depth, decompression, gas or ascent-limit guidance. The separate development sensor lab is not included in the production app or TestFlight release.
  • Reference records and saved pins remain on the device. Apple, USGS and NOAA imagery depends on provider availability, networking or available cache; no offline map imagery is guaranteed.
