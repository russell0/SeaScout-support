SeaScout pilot use notice

SeaScout is a navigation aid for planning and reviewing locations. It is not an emergency service, dive computer, chart replacement, or guarantee of a safe route. Check conditions, charts, notices, and local guidance before entering the water.

Accuracy and availability

Coordinates, map imagery, reception, device sensors, and network services can be incomplete or unavailable. Never rely on SeaScout as your only means of navigation or emergency communication.
