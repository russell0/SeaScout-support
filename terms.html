SeaScout use and source notice

Version scope: The iPhone Map tab, USGS/NOAA image layers, 90 NPS Biscayne moorings and five-area catalogue described here apply to the SeaScout 1.0 build 17 TestFlight candidate. The earlier build-14 App Review submission contains 1,575 reference records and four location packs, without those additions. This notice does not establish build-17 public availability or approval.

SeaScout is a surface navigation aid for planning and reviewing locations. It is not an emergency service, dive computer, official nautical chart, or guarantee of a safe route. Check conditions, charts, notices, and local guidance before entering the water. Use your established dive computer before descent.

Accuracy and availability

Coordinates, map imagery, reception, device sensors, and network services can be incomplete or unavailable. GPS is unavailable underwater. A straight-line bearing does not account for land, traffic, restrictions, current, or weather. Saved marks represent historical positions. Independent geographic and field review of the bundled charted points remain pending. Never rely on SeaScout as your only means of navigation or emergency communication.

Catalogue sources

The bundled reference catalogue contains 765 artificial-reef deployment records from the Florida Fish and Wildlife Conservation Commission, Fish and Wildlife Research Institute; originator: FWC Division of Marine Fisheries Management. The data are for display, not navigation, survey, or legal use. Attribution and original metadata are retained. See the FWC source metadata.

The catalogue also includes 470 published mooring-buoy records from NOAA’s Florida Keys National Marine Sanctuary: 250 in the Hollywood / Upper Keys collection and 220 at 24 source locations in the Key West / Lower Keys collection. Location-pack counts can overlap because the Upper Keys collection is also included in the Hollywood pack. The data retain their original source coordinates and identifiers from the 2025 dataset snapshot retrieved October 1, 2026; this addition is not a current-condition survey. These positions are not reef extents or wreck centers and do not establish current buoy presence, condition, availability, or access. See the NOAA mooring source and public-information notice. No NOAA endorsement is implied.

The freshwater collection includes 340 records from the Florida Department of Environmental Protection / Florida Geological Survey springs inventory: 333 spring records, six cave-associated spring positions and Orange Grove sinkhole. Selection includes current, historical or group first/second-magnitude records and specific named freshwater features; magnitude describes discharge, not depth or diving difficulty. The agency's map service reprojected native source geometry to WGS84, and returned coordinates and original source identifiers are retained. See the FGS springs programme, source inventory and full dataset limitations. The dataset is provided as a public service without accuracy or fitness guarantees; users remain responsible for use, redistribution and reproduction.

Freshwater records are not a list of universally accessible or diveable sites. Cave and sinkhole labels use linked operator or state-park descriptions, not inferred underground routes. A cave-associated source point is not a surveyed entrance or permission to enter at that point. Access, depth, accuracy and field conditions remain unknown unless a source expressly provides them. No subsidence incident reports are presented as recreational destinations. No cave-passage maps, operator photographs or logos are included, and no agency or operator endorsement is implied.

In build 17, the Biscayne collection adds 90 individual mooring positions published by the National Park Service: 21 wreck-associated, 32 reef and 37 other moorings. Original buoy names and published decimal coordinates are retained. These are buoy positions, not wreck centers, reef boundaries or surveyed dive entries. The source does not specify a horizontal datum; no transformation or source WGS84 certification is claimed. Source depth is a labeled buoy measurement, not a current site depth range. All 60 rows in the additional Ajax table were excluded because of an apparent coordinate outlier; no correction was inferred. See the NPS mooring table and Maritime Heritage Trail. Data: National Park Service, Biscayne National Park. No claim to original U.S. Government works. NPS source notice. No NPS endorsement, current buoy availability or access permission is implied.

A separate selection contains 15 points derived from original NOAA Electronic Navigational Charts. Access requires reading and accepting the NOAA ENC user agreement in the app. Original chart source, edition, and source dates remain visible. The selection is a dated subset, not a complete or official NOAA ENC, not automatically updated charts, and not a chart-carriage substitute.

No OpenDiveMap data is included in this version. The build-17 published reference catalogue totals 1,665 records, excluding the separate charted collection and private points.

Map imagery and relief — build 17 additions

Standard and Satellite imagery are supplied by Apple MapKit and remain subject to Apple's terms and availability. Saved source records are distinct from basemap imagery; no guaranteed offline imagery download is included.

Topographic imagery comes from USGS, The National Map, with land contours in covered United States areas. Map services and data available from U.S. Geological Survey, National Geospatial Program. See USGS map-service terms and the USGS Topo service.

Seafloor relief is supplied by NOAA National Centers for Environmental Information through its Global DEM Mosaic color shaded relief service. It combines models with varying resolution and different vertical datums without harmonizing them. Its source terms exclude navigation. SeaScout presents this image for discovery and planning context only and does not derive water depth, clearance or routes from its colors. Surface guidance uses source coordinates and GPS independently of the relief image. This layer is not an official nautical chart.

USGS and NOAA layers request detail only through zoom 16. Coverage, current service availability and image resolution vary. Zoom out or change the layer when imagery is unavailable. No agency endorsement is implied.
