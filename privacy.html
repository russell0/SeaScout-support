SeaScout privacy notice

Effective date: October 2, 2026

This notice describes the current SeaScout pilot published by Russell Hanson. It will be updated before any account, public sharing, or hosted backend is enabled.

What the pilot does

SeaScout displays saved reefs, wrecks, and other navigation points on iPhone and Apple Watch. The pilot does not require an account, does not provide a hosted user profile, and does not publish your saved points to other users.

Location

When you allow location access, the app uses your device location to center the map and support navigation features. Location is processed by the app on your devices. Apple MapKit may process requests needed to provide Apple maps and related services under Apple’s terms and privacy policy.

Saved data and transfer

Saved pins, imported region files, and paired-device copies are stored on your devices or transferred between your paired iPhone and Apple Watch. The pilot has no advertising SDK and does not sell personal information. We do not intentionally collect analytics or build a cross-device profile.

Retention and deletion

Device data remains until you delete it in the app, remove the app, or erase the device. See Delete local data. Because the pilot has no account service, there is no server account to close.

Contact

Questions or privacy requests: russellhanson@gmail.com.
