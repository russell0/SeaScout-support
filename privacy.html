SeaScout privacy notice

Effective date: October 2, 2026

This notice describes SeaScout 1.0, published by Russell Hanson. It will be updated before any account, public sharing, or hosted backend is enabled.

What SeaScout does

SeaScout displays reference records and private navigation points on iPhone and Apple Watch. It does not require an account, provide a hosted user profile, or publish your saved points to other users.

Location

When you allow location access, the app processes your device location and heading on your devices to center the map, save surface marks, and show surface guidance. SeaScout does not send your location or saved positions to a developer-operated server. You can browse the catalogue without location permission and change permission in your device’s Settings.

Apple MapKit may process requests needed to provide map imagery and related services under Apple Maps & Privacy. Opening a source or support link connects to that website under its own privacy practices.

Saved data and transfer

Saved pins, names, source-agreement acceptance, imported region files, and transfer status are stored on your devices or transferred between your paired iPhone and Apple Watch through WatchConnectivity. SeaScout has no advertising or analytics SDK, does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites, and does not sell personal information. No developer backend or account service is enabled.

Retention and deletion

Local data remains until you delete it in the app, remove the app, or erase the device. Delete copies on each device; removing a phone copy does not establish that a Watch copy has also been removed. See Delete local data. Device backups and any restoration are managed through your Apple device and backup settings. There is no server account to close.

Contact

Questions or privacy requests: russellhanson@gmail.com.

If you email support, we receive the email address and information you choose to include and use them to respond. Please avoid sending private coordinates or other unnecessary personal information.
