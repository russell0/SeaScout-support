SeaScout privacy notice

Effective date: October 3, 2026

This notice describes SeaScout 1.0, published by Russell Hanson. It will be updated before any account, public sharing, or hosted backend is enabled.

Version scope: The iPhone Map tab, Nearby lookup and custom USGS/NOAA imagery described below apply to the SeaScout 1.0 build 17 TestFlight candidate. The earlier build-14 App Review submission does not make these custom imagery requests. Apple MapKit, local storage and paired-device transfer apply to both versions.

What SeaScout does

SeaScout displays reference records and private navigation points on iPhone and Apple Watch. It does not require an account, provide a hosted user profile, or publish your saved points to other users.

Location

When you allow location access, the app processes your device location and heading on your devices to center the map, save surface marks, and show surface guidance. SeaScout does not send your location or saved positions to a developer-operated server. You can browse the catalogue without location permission and change permission in your device’s Settings.

In the iPhone Map tab, Nearby performs a one-time foreground lookup when you tap it. Manual browsing does not start continuous location tracking. Starting surface guidance is a separate action that uses the app's location session.

Online map imagery

Apple MapKit processes requests needed for map content and related services under Apple Maps & Privacy. Selecting Topographic or Seafloor relief additionally requests images directly from USGS or NOAA for the map area you view. Apple map content may remain beneath these layers.

USGS requests identify map tiles; NOAA requests identify geographic image bounds. Providers also receive ordinary connection information such as your IP address. SeaScout does not include your private-point names, search text or a GPS-history payload in these custom image requests. A view centered on Nearby or a private point nevertheless reveals the surrounding map area to the imagery provider. Your saved records are not published to other SeaScout users.

The custom USGS/NOAA image loader uses HTTPS without cookies or saved credentials and keeps a limited memory cache. It does not download complete offline maps. The agencies describe automatic storage of IP addresses, request times, requested information and browser/operating-system information in server logs. They use this information for service operations, security and usage analysis. These are agency logs; SeaScout does not receive them, and this notice does not promise a particular agency retention period. See USGS privacy, NOAA privacy and NOAA NCEI privacy. Opening source or support links also connects to those websites under their privacy practices.

Saved data and transfer

Saved pins, names, source-agreement acceptance, imported region files, and transfer status are stored on your devices or transferred between your paired iPhone and Apple Watch through WatchConnectivity. SeaScout has no advertising or analytics SDK, does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites, and does not sell personal information. No developer backend or account service is enabled.

Retention and deletion

Local data remains until you delete it in the app, remove the app, or erase the device. Delete copies on each device; removing a phone copy does not establish that a Watch copy has also been removed. See Delete local data. Device backups and any restoration are managed through your Apple device and backup settings. There is no server account to close.
